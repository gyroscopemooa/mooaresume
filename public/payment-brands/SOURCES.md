# Payment brand assets

- Kakao Pay: https://t1.daumcdn.net/kakaopay/developer-assets/img/main/logo-d.svg (linked by the official developer site). Retained the exact Pay symbol/wordmark paths, removed the separate developer suffix, used the standard dark foreground and fitted the viewBox. Original downloaded source preserved in the local build folder.
- N Pay: https://developers.pay.naver.com/img/logo/signature/logo_navergr_small.svg (official https://developers.pay.naver.com/design/brand/logo). Unmodified.
- Samsung Pay: https://d3unf4s5rp9dfh.cloudfront.net/SAMSUNGWALLET/img-pay-logo-type01.png, linked by https://developer.samsung.com/pay/design. Unmodified.

Brand marks identify the selected payment service. NICE retains the payment agreement and native selection step. These assets do not establish merchant availability of any additional payment method.
